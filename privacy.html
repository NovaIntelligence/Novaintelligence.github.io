Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 Oct 2025

Nova Intelligence Group (“we”, “us”, “our”) builds tools and dashboards. This policy explains what we collect, why, how we use it, and your choices. Contact: privacy@novaintelligencegroup.io.

What we collect

  • Contact data (name, email, company, messages).
  • Usage & diagnostics (IP, device/browser, pages, timestamps, error logs).
  • Integrations (only if you connect them) — e.g., Garmin Connect via OAuth/API scopes you approve; revoke anytime in the provider account.

How we use data

  • Provide/improve services; secure/monitor; support communications; legal compliance.

Legal bases

Contract performance, legitimate interests (security, improvement), consent (optional integrations/analytics), and legal obligations.

Sharing

No sale of personal data. We use vetted processors (hosting, analytics, error tracking, support) under data protection agreements; lawful disclosures as required.

International transfers

Where required, we use safeguards (e.g., SCCs) and technical controls.

Retention

Kept only as long as needed, then deleted or de-identified. You can request deletion anytime.

Security

Encryption in transit, access controls, least privilege, logging. Report issues: security@novaintelligencegroup.io.

Your rights

  • Access/correct/delete your data; revoke integrations; object/restrict; opt out of marketing.
  • Region-specific rights (GDPR/UK, CCPA/CPRA, etc.) may apply.

Request: privacy@novaintelligencegroup.io.
